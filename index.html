What ClinSync AI Does

  • Reconciles information across synthetic sources with source priority
  • Detects contradictions, missing fields, ambiguity, outdated records
  • Retrieves supporting evidence from available synthetic records
  • Generates structured follow-up with full traceability
  • Runs 10 validation checks before finalization
  • Escalates consequential uncertainty to human review
  • Adapts when new information is introduced — with before/after diff
